# Inventory naming recommendations (no changes applied)

Scope: full RepairDesk inventory (~2927 items). Screen/TFT work already done. This doc is **advice only**.

Goal for a third-party repair shop in NZ:

1. **Compliance** — do not imply you are Apple/Samsung or that every part is brand-original unless it is.
2. **Advantage** — keep grade differences clear (OEM vs RHD vs LCD/OLED), make search/ticket picking fast, look professional to customers.

---

## Recommended naming formula

```text
[Grade] [Part type] for [Device] [Color/options]
```

| Element | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grade | `OEM`, `RHD`, `OLED`, `LCD` | Keep — this is your product differentiation |
| Part type | Screen, Battery, Charging Port, Back Glass, Camera Glass Lens, Ear Speaker | One consistent dictionary |
| Device | `iPhone 15 Pro`, `Samsung Galaxy A54 5G`, `Huawei P30 Pro` | Compatibility reference is OK |
| Avoid leading with brand as seller | Prefer `Battery for iPhone 15` over `Apple iPhone 15 Battery` | Reads as “compatible part”, not “Apple-sold part” |

**OK to keep brand in the device slot** (`for Samsung Galaxy S23`). That is normal repair-industry wording.

**Be careful with** `Genuine Apple…`, `Samsung Genuine…`, `OEM Apple…` on accessories unless stock is truly OEM and you want that claim on invoices.

## Priority 1 — Samsung phone parts (highest volume left)

~140+ part titles still use `Samsung … Battery/Port/Lens` instead of `… for Samsung …`.

| Current (examples) | Suggested |
|---|---|
| `Samsung Galaxy A10 Battery` | `Battery for Samsung Galaxy A10` |
| `Samsung A34 charging port` | `Charging Port for Samsung Galaxy A34` |
| `Samsung Galaxy A10 Earspeaker` | `Ear Speaker for Samsung Galaxy A10` |
| `Samsung Galaxy A23 Camera Lens` | `Camera Glass Lens for Samsung Galaxy A23` |
| `Samsung Phone A70 Ear Speaker` | `Ear Speaker for Samsung Galaxy A70` |
| `Samsung Phone S8/S8 Plus Home Button (FingerPrint)` | `Home Button for Samsung Galaxy S8 / S8 Plus` |
| `Samsung S8 Charging Port` | `Charging Port for Samsung Galaxy S8` |
| `OEM Screen for Samsung A34 with Frame` | keep OEM; optionally `OEM Screen for Samsung Galaxy A34 with Frame` |

### Samsung consistency rules (advantage)

| Issue now | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Mix of `Samsung Phone S10`, `Samsung S10`, `Samsung Galaxy S10` | Standardise on **`Samsung Galaxy …`** for phones |
| Mix of `Earspeaker` / `Ear Speaker` / `EarSpeaker` | Always **`Ear Speaker`** |
| Mix of `Charging Port` / `Chargerport` / `charging port` | Always **`Charging Port`** |
| Mix of `Camera Lens` / `Lens` / `Camera Glass Lens` | Always **`Camera Glass Lens`** (glass only) vs full camera module if different SKU |
| `A21S` vs `A21s` | Pick one (prefer manufacturer style: **A21s**) |
| `S20FE` vs `S20 FE` | Always **`S20 FE`** |
| Duplicate refurbished listings (many identical A32 / S10e rows) | Merge or archive duplicates — cleaner stock & less wrong picks |

**Do not rename** real Samsung retail goods that are not repair parts (EVO microSD, OEM SSD, appliances). Those can stay brand-leading.

## Priority 4 — OEM / Genuine accessories (compliance-sensitive)

These are **not** phone screens; claims are stronger here.

| Current | Risk | Suggested if aftermarket | Suggested if true OEM stock |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Genuine Apple 60W MagSafe Adapter…` | Strong authenticity claim | `Power Adapter for MacBook MagSafe 60W` | `OEM Power Adapter for MacBook MagSafe 60W` |
| `OEM Apple 45W Magsafe…` | “OEM Apple” reads like Apple-branded OEM | `OEM Power Adapter for MacBook Air MagSafe 45W` | same, keep OEM only if verified |
| `OEM Aftermarket 87W USB-C…` | Contradictory (`OEM` + `Aftermarket`) | Pick one: `Aftermarket Power Adapter…` **or** `OEM Power Adapter…` | — |
| `Samsung Genuine Headset…` | Strong claim | `Headset for Samsung phones…` | `OEM Headset for Samsung…` |
| `…OEM Earpod Earphone for iPhone…` | “Earpod” echoes AirPods trademark vibe | `OEM Earphones for iPhone / iPad (Lightning)` | keep OEM if true |
| `Genuine Leather Band Strap for Apple Watch` | “Genuine” = leather material, usually OK | Optional: `Leather Band for Apple Watch Black` | — |

**Advantage:** one grade language everywhere — **OEM** (your higher tier) vs no prefix / RHD / Aftermarket — instead of mixing Genuine / Org / Original / OEM Aftermarket.

---

## Priority 5 — Used / refurbished devices (keep brand-leading)

Whole devices **should** keep the brand name in the title:

- Good: `Refurbished Samsung Galaxy A54 5G 128GB Black`
- Prefer one template: `[Condition] [Brand] [Model] [Storage] [Colour]`
- Avoid mixing `Refurbished` suffix vs prefix, and clean duplicate SKUs (many identical A32 / S10e rows)

This is different from **parts**: devices are the product; parts are compatible replacements.

## Compliance vs advantage — short summary

| Topic | Compliance | Business advantage |
|---|---|---|
| `Part for Device` | Reads as third-party compatible | Faster search; clearer quotes |
| Keep `OEM` / `RHD` / `OLED`/`LCD` | Grade is factual shop language | Upsell path; fewer wrong picks |
| Avoid `Genuine Apple` / `Samsung Genuine` unless true | Lower trademark/misleading risk | Still can say OEM when you mean OEM grade |
| Standardise Galaxy / spelling | Neutral | Fewer duplicates, cleaner staff training |
| Keep brand on refurbished phones | Accurate product identity | Trust + SEO for used listings |
